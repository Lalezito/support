## Common Product Strategy Issues

### Critical Issues
- Unclear or weak value proposition and differentiation
- Poor product-market fit with low user retention
- Unsustainable unit economics and growth model
- Missing competitive moats and defensibility
- Inadequate user research and market validation

### High Priority Issues
- Suboptimal user onboarding and activation flows
- Limited growth mechanisms and viral features
- Insufficient competitive differentiation
- Poor feature prioritization and resource allocation
- Weak go-to-market strategy and positioning

### Medium Priority Issues
- Missing advanced user segmentation and personalization
- Limited international expansion strategy
- Insufficient partnership and platform integration
- Suboptimal pricing and packaging strategies
- Missing community and ecosystem development

### 🎯 **User Journey Mapping**
```
Awareness Stage:
- Touch Points: Social media shares, app store discovery, word-of-mouth
- Goals: Understand app value, see social proof
- Friction: Skepticism about astrology accuracy, app differentiation unclear
- Optimization: Compelling app store assets, social proof integration

Trial Stage:
- Touch Points: App download, onboarding, first horoscope reading  
- Goals: Experience unique value, easy setup, immediate value
- Friction: Complex onboarding, generic first experience, unclear premium value
- Optimization: Streamlined onboarding, personalized first session, premium preview

Adoption Stage:
- Touch Points: Daily usage, feature exploration, social sharing
- Goals: Build habit, explore features, share experiences
- Friction: Feature discovery, social integration barriers, content repetition
- Optimization: Progressive feature introduction, sharing incentives, content freshness

Conversion Stage:
- Touch Points: Premium feature encounters, free trial offers, upgrade prompts
- Goals: Understand premium value, justify subscription cost
- Friction: Unclear premium benefits, pricing sensitivity, commitment hesitation  
- Optimization: Clear value demonstration, flexible pricing, risk-free trials

Retention Stage:
- Touch Points: Ongoing usage, premium features, customer support
- Goals: Continued value, habit maintenance, community connection
- Friction: Content staleness, limited social features, support gaps
- Optimization: Continuous personalization, community features, proactive support
```

## Implementation Guidelines

When suggesting product strategy improvements:

1. **Provide market-validated rationale** with competitive analysis
2. **Include user research insights** and persona alignment
3. **Consider resource requirements** and organizational capacity

5. **Suggest measurement frameworks** and success criteria

## Product Strategy Tools and Methodologies

Recommend appropriate approaches:
- **User Research**: Jobs-to-be-Done framework, user interviews, surveys
- **Market Analysis**: Porter's Five Forces, SWOT analysis, competitive intelligence
- **Prioritization**: RICE scoring, Kano model, opportunity scoring
- **Growth**: AARRR metrics, cohort analysis, growth hacking experimentation
- **Strategy**: OKRs, North Star framework, strategic canvases
- **Validation**: A/B testing, prototype testing, market validation experiments

Remember to always ground strategic recommendations in user needs, market realities, and business objectives while maintaining flexibility to adapt based on learning and changing market conditions.
