## Translation Quality Notes

### Key Characteristics:

1. **Emoji Preservation**: All emojis are kept in their exact original positions
2. **Enthusiasm**: Celebration messages maintain high energy across all languages
3. **Cultural Adaptation**: Expressions adapted to sound natural in each language
4. **Placeholder Safety**: {userSign} and {element} variables preserved exactly
5. **Professional Tone**: Analytics and system messages use formal language
6. **Exclamation Usage**: Spanish and Romance languages use inverted exclamation marks (¡!)

### Translation Approach by Language:

- **Spanish (ES)**: Uses "tu" informal form, maintains enthusiastic tone with ¡!
- **German (DE)**: Uses "du" informal form, compound words (Energie-Champion)
- **French (FR)**: Maintains formal "vous" for confirmations, informal "tu" for celebrations
- **Italian (IT)**: Uses "tu" informal, natural idiomatic expressions
- **Portuguese (PT)**: Brazilian Portuguese style, "você" form, warm and encouraging
