Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 16, 2025

📋 Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide personalized astrological services:

  • Birth Information: Birth date and time for astrological calculations
  • Personal Goals: Goals and journal entries you choose to save
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymized app usage data to improve our services
  • Device Information: Device type and OS version for compatibility
  • Subscription Data: Payment information processed through Apple

🎯 How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide personalized horoscopes and astrological insights
  • Generate compatibility analyses between zodiac signs
  • Track your personal goals and progress
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Process subscription payments through Apple's App Store
  • Provide customer support when you reach out

🔒 Information Sharing

We do NOT share:

  • Your personal astrological readings
  • Journal entries or personal goals
  • Individual compatibility analyses
  • Personal profile information with third parties

We may share anonymized, aggregated usage statistics solely for app improvement purposes.

🛡️ Data Security

We protect your data with:

  • Industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL)
  • Secure cloud storage infrastructure
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access controls for our team
  • No third-party data sales - ever

✅ Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export: Export your journal entries and saved content
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics collection in app settings
  • Contact: Reach out with any privacy concerns

🍪 Cookies and Tracking

Our mobile app does not use cookies. We use minimal analytics to understand app usage patterns, which can be disabled in your device settings.

👶 Children's Privacy

Zodiac Life Coach is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or via email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
