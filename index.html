❓ FAQ

How do I change my zodiac sign?

Subscriptions are managed via App Store. Go to iPhone Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions.
What does AI Astrology include?
Unlimited access to Cosmic Coach with GPT-4 and deep personalized analysis.
Can I restore my purchases?
Yes, go to Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases.
