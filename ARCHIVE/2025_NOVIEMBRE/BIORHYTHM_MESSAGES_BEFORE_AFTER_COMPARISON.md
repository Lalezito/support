## 🌍 TRANSLATION NOTES

### Cultural Adaptations

#### Spanish
- Uses "tú" form (informal) for warmth and personal connection
- "Honra" (honor) maintains the spiritual/respectful tone

#### Portuguese
- Uses "você" form (polite informal) for Brazilian Portuguese
- "Honre" maintains the respectful imperative

#### French
- Uses "vous" form (formal) for respectful tone
- "Honorez" maintains the elevated spiritual language

#### German
- Uses "du" form (informal) for personal connection
- Compound words like "Stier-Weisheit" (Taurus-wisdom) follow German convention
- "Ehre" (honor) maintains the spiritual tone

#### Italian
- Uses "tu" form (informal) for warmth
- "Onora" maintains the respectful tone
- "dono" (gift) is more poetic than "regalo" for sensitivity
