After thorough investigation, I discovered that **"ritual" is NOT a separate functionality or button in the Goals section**. ## Key Findings

"Ritual" appears in **Cosmic Coach suggested goals** as part of goal titles. It is not:
- ❌ A separate button or action
- ❌ A completion method
- ❌ A premium/free feature toggle
- ❌ A special animation or ceremony

**It IS:**
- ✅ Part of goal titles generated by the Cosmic Coach AI
- ✅ A descriptive word for certain wellness/spiritual goals
- ✅ Already properly translated in both English and Spanish

## 6. Is This a Bug?

### Answer: NO

This is **not a bug**. The confusion arises from:

1. **Mixed Language Display**: If Spanish goals appear with "Ritual de..." users might think it's a button label
2. **Lack of Clarity**: The word "ritual" sounds like an action, but it's just a goal category/theme

### Recommendation:
Consider this a **UX clarity issue**, not a functionality bug. The feature works as designed.
